Result interpretation

How to read these results

Isolation Forest detects patterns that differ from learned normal behavior. It does not directly prove that a mechanical component has failed.

Diagnostic suggestions are rule-based support and require human verification. Treat anomalies as “inspect this area” signals, not definitive fault identification.
